Corrections Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026 • Netflix4U Data Accuracy Desk

Maintaining an accurate and trustworthy database of more than 30,000 films and television programs is an ongoing responsibility. Despite rigorous ingestion checks, errors, typos, or outdated streaming availability can occasionally arise. Netflix4U is committed to acknowledging and swiftly correcting verifiable inaccuracies.

1. Types of Reportable Inaccuracies

We actively welcome reports regarding:

  • Cast and Crew Errors: Misattributed actors, missing directors, or incorrect character names.
  • Release Dates and Timeline Discrepancies: Incorrect theatrical premiere years or misleading episode air dates.
  • Technical Specifications: Inaccurate runtimes, incorrect original audio language, or faulty aspect ratios.
  • Streaming Availability & Broken Links: Mirror links that no longer resolve, expired cloud tokens, or mislabeled audio dubs.
  • Artwork & Poster Mismatch: Poster or backdrop image belonging to a different film with a similar name.

2. How to Submit a Correction Request

To ensure prompt review by our catalog team, please email your report to our dedicated accuracy desk:

Netflix4U Corrections & Data Verification Desk

Email: corrections@netflix4u.in

• Subject line: [Correction Request] Title Name (Year) - Specific Issue

• Page URL: Include the exact URL on netflix4u.in where the error appears.

• Correction Details: Explain what is incorrect and provide the accurate information.

• Source/Reference: Provide a link to an official studio site, TMDB, or IMDb entry to accelerate verification.

3. Review & Resolution Process

Our editorial team evaluates every submission against primary registry databases. Verified corrections are updated directly in our master catalog within 24 to 48 hours. When structural changes are made to a title, the modification timestamp is refreshed to preserve transparency for our users and automated systems.

4. Community Gratitude

We are deeply grateful to film buffs, researchers, and community members who help keep Netflix4U the cleanest, most reliable media discovery portal on the web.
