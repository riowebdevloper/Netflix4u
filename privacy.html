Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

At Netflix4U (accessible from https://netflix4u.in/), protecting the privacy and rights of our global visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Netflix4U and how we responsibly utilize it.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Netflix4U is designed as a free, open-access media catalog. We do not require user registration, login credentials, payment details, phone numbers, or credit card information. You can discover, search, and browse our catalog with complete anonymity.

2. Log Files & Standard Technical Data

Like almost all modern web servers, Netflix4U follows standard logging procedures. The information collected by server log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These technical logs are used solely for analyzing trends, preventing DDoS attacks, administering the platform, and tracking aggregate traffic flow. None of this data is linked to any personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies and Web Storage

Netflix4U utilizes modern browser local storage and session storage for essential client-side functionality, such as:

  • Remembering your preferred audio, subtitle, or video server settings.
  • Remembering "Continue Watching" playback positions locally on your own device.
  • Storing dismissed promotional or sticky notification states.

You can choose to disable cookies or clear local storage through your individual browser settings at any time without losing the ability to browse the catalog.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics Partners

To keep our discovery portal freely accessible, Netflix4U may display non-intrusive advertisements served by verified third-party ad networks (such as Google AdSense, A-ADS, or Adsterra) and utilize privacy-conscious telemetry (Google Analytics 4). These third-party vendors may use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and customize content.

Netflix4U has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers. We encourage you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information and opt-out instructions.

5. CCPA & GDPR Rights

Under applicable data protection regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), users have the right to request information on what data is held and request its deletion. Because Netflix4U does not maintain user accounts or personal profiles, we do not retain identifiable personal databases. If you contact our support desk via email, your email address is used solely to respond to your specific inquiry and is never shared, sold, or rented.

6. Children's Information

Protecting children while using the internet is an essential priority. Netflix4U does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

7. Consent & Updates

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated modification date.
