DMCA Copyright Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Netflix4U respects the intellectual property rights of creators, producers, film studios, and copyright holders. It is our policy to respond expeditiously to legitimate claims of copyright infringement pursuant to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and international intellectual property standards.

1. Nature of the Service

Netflix4U operates as an informational search engine, catalog directory, and metadata aggregator. We index publicly available metadata, synopses, promotional trailers, and external third-party links. Netflix4U does not store, host, or broadcast copyrighted audiovisual media files directly on its web application servers.

2. Notice and Takedown Procedure

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent thereof and believe that any content, link, or metadata indexed on Netflix4U infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a formal notification with the following information:

  • A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple copyrighted works are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works.
  • Identification of the specific URL(s) on Netflix4U where the allegedly infringing link or entry is located.
  • Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

3. Designated Copyright Agent

Please send all DMCA notices and copyright communications to our designated compliance desk:

Netflix4U Copyright Compliance Agent

Email: dmca@netflix4u.in

Response Window: All verified notices are reviewed and processed within 24 to 72 business hours.

4. Action Upon Valid Notice

Upon receipt of a valid and complete notification, Netflix4U will promptly remove or disable access to the identified URL(s), purge the corresponding catalog links from our active index, and notify any relevant upstream third-party sources where applicable.

5. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable law, Netflix4U has adopted a policy of terminating or blacklisting links from third-party sources or providers that are repeatedly identified as infringing.
